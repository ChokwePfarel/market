The moments

Little pieces of us

When life gets difficult

When the storm comes

There will be days when life feels heavy. Days when the road gets difficult, when words fail us, when we don't understand each other, and when the world seems to ask more from us than we have to give.

There will be storms we never expected. Moments when the sky turns dark and the future feels uncertain.

But I don't want a love that only exists in the sunshine.

I want the kind of love that stays when the rain comes. The kind that reaches for your hand when everything feels dark. The kind that listens when words are difficult, forgives when hearts are tired, and chooses to try again.

Because storms don't mean the journey is over. Sometimes they simply remind us how precious the light is.

If we ever lose our way,
I hope we find our way back to each other like we always do.

And if the world becomes loud, I hope you remember this:

I am still here.

Not only for the easy days. Not only for the laughter and beautiful moments.

For the storms too.

Because what we have is worth fighting for. And when the storm finally passes, I want to be standing beside you, looking at the same sky, grateful that we stayed.

❤️

Still you. Still us.

Real love isn't the absence of storms. It's choosing each other while the storm is passing.

Always, with all my heart.
